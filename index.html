What AgenticScore is

A reputation layer for Binance Agentic Wallets. Wallet activity is measured, converted into a score, and written to chain as a non-transferable NFT. For the MVP, only wallets on the project allowlist can take part.

Five inputs decide the score

The score measures how active an Agent Wallet is, including the total number and the frequency of its activities. More legitimate activity means a higher potential score.

Activity count
Total number of recorded Agent Wallet activities. This is the heaviest input.
Activity frequency
How often the wallet performs activities.
Activity consistency
How steadily the wallet stays active over time.
Activity recency
How recently the wallet last acted.
Activity diversity
How many different kinds of activity the wallet performs.

What keeps a score honest

  • Activity is validated before it counts toward a score.
  • Duplicate activity is discarded.
  • Anti-abuse checks run against the scoring engine.
  • Only eligible and verifiable Agent Wallet activity contributes.
  • Weights and thresholds stay configurable by the project administrator.

Non-transferable by design

A soulbound token has no normal transfer path. There is nothing to sell and no way to move a reputation into someone else's wallet.

  • Cannot be transferred between wallets through normal transfer functions.
  • Stays attached to the wallet that minted it.
  • Stands for verified participation and activity reputation, not a tradable asset.
  • Enforced by the smart contract.

Eligibility and wallet verification

For the MVP, eligibility is managed through an operator-controlled allowlist. A wallet has to be on that list before it can mint.

  1. Submit your Binance Agentic Wallet address.
  2. The system checks whether the wallet is on the approved allowlist.
  3. You sign a verification message with your wallet.
  4. The backend verifies the signature and wallet ownership.
  5. Eligible wallets continue to minting.

AgenticScore never asks for:

  • Private keys
  • Seed phrases
  • Wallet passwords

Verification is a wallet address plus a signature you approve yourself.

What the signature step does

After the allowlist check, your wallet signs a plain verification message. Signing proves you control the address. The backend checks that signature, and only then does mint authorization open. Signing a message does not move funds and does not reveal a key.

Official Binance attestation may be added in a later version, subject to availability and technical compatibility.

Questions this project raises

Short answers to the things people ask first.

Is AgenticScore an official Binance product?

No. AgenticScore is an independent project. It is not an official Binance product unless explicitly announced otherwise. Binance-related wallet verification and activity integrations depend on technical availability, compatibility, and platform permissions.

Can I transfer or sell the NFT?

No. The token is soulbound. It cannot be transferred between wallets through normal transfer functions and it stays attached to the wallet that minted it.

What does minting cost?

The project charges no minting fee. You pay the blockchain gas fee for your own transaction on BNB Smart Chain.

Will you ever ask for my private key or seed phrase?

No. Verification uses your wallet address and a signature you approve. Private keys, seed phrases, and wallet passwords are never requested or stored.

How is my rarity tier decided?

Your Agentic Activity Score decides the tier. The administrator configures where each tier starts, and those thresholds are not published on this page.
