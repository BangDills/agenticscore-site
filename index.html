What AgenticScore is

A reputation layer for Binance Agentic Wallets. Wallet activity is measured and turned into a score that ranks you on the leaderboard. Joining takes one non-transferable NFT, identical for every wallet. During the MVP, only wallets on the project allowlist can take part.

What counts toward your score

Your score comes from verified activity in your wallet. Each kind of activity earns points, but every extra repeat of the same kind adds a little less than the last. Spam barely helps, real activity still moves you up, and there is no maximum score.

Token swaps
Weighted highest.
NFT mints
Weighted in the middle, with diminishing returns.
Transfers
Weighted lowest, because they are cheap to repeat.
Active days
Rewards steady use over short bursts.

How scores stay honest

  • Each transaction counts once.
  • Transfers between your own wallets are ignored.
  • Dust-value swaps and transfers are ignored.
  • Only activity inside the current 7-day window counts.

Mint with your own agent

Copy this prompt into the agent that controls your Binance Agentic Wallet. It tells the agent to check the official contract first, ask for your approval before anything costs gas, mint once, and tell you your score.

Minting is not open. The prompt only works after a contract address is published on this site.

Prompt for your agent 
You are acting for me with my Binance Agentic Wallet. Your goal is to mint my AgenticScore soulbound NFT on BNB Smart Chain, once.

My wallet address: [PASTE YOUR AGENTIC WALLET ADDRESS]

Before you do anything:
1. Open https://agenticscore.xyz and confirm that minting is open and that an official contract address is published. If there is none, stop and tell me.
2. Check my wallet address against the allowlist on the site. If it is not eligible, stop and tell me.

Then:
3. Show me the verification message and sign it with my wallet. Do not sign anything else.
4. Show me the gas estimate and wait for my approval before you send the transaction.
5. Mint exactly one AgenticScore NFT. If the transaction fails, report the error and do not retry on your own.
6. Report the transaction hash and the token ID.
7. Look up my wallet on the leaderboard at https://agenticscore.xyz and tell me my current score and rank. If my wallet is not listed yet, say so instead of guessing.

Rules:
- Never ask for or reveal private keys, seed phrases, or passwords.
- Only interact with the contract address published on agenticscore.xyz.
- Do not send funds anywhere except the gas for this one transaction.

Leaderboard

Wallets are ranked by Agentic Activity Score. Every 7 days, the top 100 share a 1,000 USDT reward pool.

  • Rankings are taken at the end of each 7-day snapshot, and rewards are distributed then.
  • Rewards are paid from a separate wallet, not from the NFT contract.
  • The first snapshot date and the minimum score to qualify for a reward are not announced yet.
  • Only wallets that minted an AgenticScore NFT are ranked.
  • If two wallets tie, the one that minted its AgenticScore NFT first ranks higher.

Frequently asked questions

What is AgenticScore?

A reputation score for your Agentic Wallets. It scores the activity your agent wallet performs, such as NFT mints, swaps and transfers, and ranks that wallet on the leaderboard.

Can I transfer or sell the NFT?

No. The token is soulbound and stays in the wallet that minted it.

What does minting cost?

The project charges no fee. You pay the gas fee for your own transaction on BNB Smart Chain.

How is my score calculated?

From your wallet's swaps, NFT mints, transfers, and active days. Each type earns points with diminishing returns, so repeating one action adds less each time. There is no maximum score, and only the current 7-day window counts. Exact point values are not published.

Who gets the USDT reward?

The 100 wallets with the highest scores at the end of each 7-day snapshot share that week’s 1,000 USDT pool by rank band: 100 USDT for first place down to 5 USDT for ranks 31 to 100. Rewards are sent from a separate wallet when the snapshot ends, not by the NFT contract.
