Privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

The short version: this site collects nothing about you. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no tracking, and no mailing list.

What the site stores in your browser

One value in sessionStorage, named agenticscore-intro-seen. It stops the entry animation from repeating inside the same tab and is cleared when you close that tab. Nothing else is stored, and nothing is read back out.

The allowlist check

This site has no wallet checker and asks you for no address. Eligibility is decided by the verification backend at mint time: the agent you use sends the wallet address to it, and the backend answers whether that wallet is on the allowlist and what its starting score is. Your address reaches that backend through your agent, not through this site.

Requests other companies see

Two third parties are involved in serving these pages:

  • Google Fonts serves the Archivo and IBM Plex Mono webfonts, so Google receives your IP address and user agent.
  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps standard request logs, such as IP address, user agent, time, and path, for security and operations.

On chain

When minting opens, your mint and your score are public on BNB Smart Chain, tied to your wallet address, and permanent. That is how a public blockchain works. The project cannot hide or delete that data.

What the project does not do

  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No profiling and no advertising.
  • No tracking across other sites.
  • No email list to join.

Your choices

You can block the font requests, and the page falls back to system fonts. You can use a wallet that is not tied to your identity. You can reach the site through a VPN.

Changes to this page

This page can change. The date at the top moves when it does.
