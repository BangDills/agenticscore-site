What AgenticScore is

AgenticScore measures the activity of a Binance Agentic Wallet, turns that activity into a score, and issues one soulbound NFT per eligible wallet. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Binance, Tether, or BNB Chain. Names and marks belong to their owners.

Current status

The contract is deployed on BNB Smart Chain at 0x9ABB0c362B6F907b9Dadb8941E5C04B0705e7EF0, and its source is published and verified on BscScan and Sourcify. No NFT has been minted and no reward has been paid. Every page that touches minting says so, and will keep saying so until that changes.

Your wallet and your keys

You are the only person with access to your wallet. The project never asks for a private key, a seed phrase, or a password, and never will. Minting costs 0.00008 BNB, plus the network gas for your own transaction. The 0.00008 BNB is the project's mint fee; the gas goes to the network's validators. Anyone who asks you for a key, or for a payment that is not gas, is not this project.

The NFT

  • The token is soulbound. It cannot be transferred or sold.
  • Every AgenticScore NFT is identical. There are no rarity tiers.
  • One wallet holds one NFT, and only wallets on the allowlist can mint.
  • The contract enforces a hard cap of 3,000, and minting ends when the cap is reached.

Score and rewards

The score is computed from activity inside the current 7-day window, with diminishing returns for each kind of activity. The reward pool is a separate program, paid from a separate wallet and not by the NFT contract. The rank bands published on this site are the intended split, not a promise. The first snapshot date and the minimum score to qualify are not announced yet, and the project may change or end the reward program.
